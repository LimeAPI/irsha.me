02 / The arc

Website first.
Systems next. AI last.

In that order, always. Most businesses try to buy the third step first and then wonder why nothing changes. The website is the front door; the systems are the plumbing; AI is what you add once the pipes hold.

01

Presence

The front door

Positioning, website, and the first eight seconds. If a stranger cannot tell what you do, who it is for and how to buy in under ten seconds, nothing further down the page matters. This is the medium everything else travels through.

02

Operations

The plumbing

Bookings, orders, payments, stock, reporting — the places where the hours disappear. We connect what you already own before we replace anything, because half the system is usually already paid for.

03

Intelligence

The leverage

Assistants that answer from your own documents, triage that drafts before a human reads it, workflows that stop being a person's job. AI on top of messy systems only makes faster mess.

06 / Questions

The ones
worth asking.

Do we have to replace the tools we already use?

No. We integrate first and replace only what is actively costing you time or money. Most businesses already own half the system they need — it just isn't connected.

How long does a website or storefront take?

Two to four weeks for a focused build, depending on how much content exists. Larger commerce builds with many products take longer, and we scope that after the audit.

Is our business data safe if we use AI?

Assistants are grounded in your own documents using retrieval rather than fine-tuning, access follows least-privilege, your data is never used to train public models, and a person stays in the loop on anything that leaves the building.

What does it cost?

The audit has a fixed fee and a written summary. After that we quote a fixed scope for the pilot or build, so you are never paying against an open-ended hourly meter.

Who owns the code, accounts and content?

You do. Domains, hosting, source code and every third-party account are registered in your name from day one, and we hand over documentation at the end.
